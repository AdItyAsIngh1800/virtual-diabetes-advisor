Privacy Policy

Virtual Diabetes Advisor · Team 6, B.Tech Machine Learning mini project, SRM Institute of Science and Technology · Last updated 15 November 2026

Virtual Diabetes Advisor is a student research prototype. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. It is used only by the project team and invited test users.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the name on your WhatsApp profile.
  • Health profile answers you give us (for example age, sex, blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI and general health questions).
  • Glucose readings you send, and the messages and voice notes you exchange with the advisor.
  • Delivery status of messages we send you.

How we use it

  • To calculate your personal glucose thresholds and send you advisories when a reading is close to or past them.
  • To answer your questions and connect you to a practitioner when you ask or when a reading needs attention.
  • To audit and improve the advisor's answers. Conversations are reviewed by the project team.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the project except the service providers below.

Where it is stored and processed

  • Messages travel through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta.
  • Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database with access restricted to the project team.
  • Questions may be processed by an AI model provider to draft answers; only the content needed to answer is sent.

How long we keep it

Data is kept only for the duration of the project and its evaluation, and is deleted when the project ends. Conversation audit records are kept until then so the advisor's answers can be reviewed.

Your choices

  • You can stop at any time by sending STOP to the advisor or simply not replying.
  • You can ask for your data to be deleted by sending DELETE MY DATA to the advisor on WhatsApp, or by contacting the project team. We will remove it within 30 days.
