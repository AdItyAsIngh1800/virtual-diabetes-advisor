Privacy Policy

Virtual Diabetes Advisor · Team 6, B.Tech Machine Learning mini project, SRM Institute of Science and Technology · Last updated 15 November 2026

Virtual Diabetes Advisor is a student research prototype delivered as a web app. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. It is used only by the project team and invited test users.

What we collect

  • Your account email and password, handled by Supabase Auth. We never see your password.
  • Health profile answers you give us (for example age, sex, blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI and general health questions).
  • Glucose readings you enter, and the text and voice messages you exchange with the advisor in the app.
  • If you turn on alerts: a push subscription for your browser or phone (an address at your browser's push service plus encryption keys) and the type of browser you use.
  • Whether each alert was sent and whether you opened it.

How we use it

  • To calculate your personal glucose thresholds and alert you when a reading is close to or past them.
  • To answer your questions and connect you to a practitioner when you ask or when a reading needs attention.
  • To audit and improve the advisor's answers. Conversations are reviewed by the project team.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the project except the service providers below.

Notifications

  • Alerts are sent with Web Push only after you press Enable alerts and allow notifications in your browser.
  • An alert is delivered through your browser's push service (Google for Chrome on Android, Mozilla for Firefox, Apple for Safari). The content is encrypted end to end; the push service sees only that a message was sent to your device.
  • An alert contains only your reading and your personal limit, never your name or anyone else's data. Alerts older than one hour are not delivered.

Where it is stored and processed

  • Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. Row-level security lets each patient read only their own records; practitioners see only patients assigned to them.
  • Questions may be processed by an AI model provider to draft answers; only the content needed to answer is sent.

How long we keep it

Data is kept only for the duration of the project and its evaluation, and is deleted when the project ends. Conversation audit records are kept until then so the advisor's answers can be reviewed.

Your choices

  • You can turn alerts off at any time with Turn alerts off in the app, or by blocking notifications for the site in your browser settings.
  • You can ask for your account and data to be deleted by contacting the project team. We will remove it within 30 days.
